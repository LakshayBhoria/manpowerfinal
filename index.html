A recruitment desk that knows the work

Founded in 2021 in Greater Noida, S.N. Enterprises began with a team of 20 electricians for a single contractor. Today we recruit, test, train and mobilise thousands of workers every year.

Our mission is simple: give employers dependable people, and give workers fair, safe and documented jobs.

  • In-house trade testing and safety induction
  • Payroll, insurance and accommodation support
  • Dedicated account manager for every client
